# Catch-up bookkeeping handoff checklist

Use this checklist with a bookkeeper to turn incomplete records into an agreed project scope.

## Before requesting a quote

- [ ] List the first and last month in scope and the reporting deadline.
- [ ] Identify the last fully reconciled month and the source of the opening balance for each account.
- [ ] Inventory business bank accounts, credit cards, payment processors, loans, and closed accounts active during the period.
- [ ] For each account and month, note whether the statement is available and whether the transaction count is confirmed or unknown.
- [ ] Identify months with missing entries, duplicates, uncategorized activity, unmatched transfers, or incomplete reconciliation.
- [ ] Flag periods already closed or delivered to a tax preparer. Agree on who approves any changes to those periods.
- [ ] Use aliases in the shared scope. Share statements and private financial records only through the agreed secure channel.

## Confirm the quote in writing

- [ ] Specify which accounts and months include recording, corrections, and reconciliation.
- [ ] Separate catch-up work from ongoing monthly bookkeeping and from optional cleanup.
- [ ] Record any transaction or account limits, included fees, and how the provider prices work outside the scope.
- [ ] For missing statements, agree on who will obtain each record, what can proceed, and whether the deadline or fee may change.
- [ ] Confirm the reporting periods, accounting basis, delivery format, and approval process for extra work.

## Review deliverables at handoff

- [ ] Profit and loss statement for the agreed period, with monthly detail when requested.
- [ ] Balance sheet as of the agreed date and explanations for unresolved balances.
- [ ] Reconciliation reports and a list of the source statements used.
- [ ] General ledger export, adjustment or correction log, and a list of open items.
- [ ] For each open item, record what remains unresolved, who owns the next step, and when it should be reviewed.
